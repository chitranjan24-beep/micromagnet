A spin in a field

A spin-½ in a static field along z has energies ±EZ/2 with EZ = gµBB, and precesses at the Larmor frequency fL = EZ/h. A state cos(θ/2)|↓⟩ + eiφ sin(θ/2)|↑⟩ is a point on the Bloch sphere at polar angle θ and azimuth φ; the measured spin-up probability is sin²(θ/2).

A transverse field oscillating at frequency f with amplitude B1 is, in the frame rotating at f, a static field of amplitude B1/2 in the equatorial plane plus a field along z proportional to the detuning Δ = fL − f. The spin rotates about their sum at the generalized Rabi frequency √(fR² + Δ²), with fR = gµBB1/(2h):

\[ P_\uparrow(t) = \frac{f_R^2}{f_R^2 + \Delta^2}\,\sin^2\!\left(\pi\sqrt{f_R^2+\Delta^2}\,t\right). \]

The phase of the drive sets the direction of the rotation axis in the equatorial plane. Rotations about z are equivalent to shifting the phase of all later drive pulses.

Fidelities and benchmarking

  • State fidelity to a pure target: F = ⟨ψ|ρ|ψ⟩.
  • Readout: visibility V = F0 + F1 − 1; average readout fidelity (F0 + F1)/2.
  • Randomized benchmarking: F(m) = Apm + B; average Clifford fidelity FC = 1 − (1 − p)(d − 1)/d, with d = 2 for one qubit and 4 for two.
  • Average gate fidelity and process (entanglement) fidelity: Favg = (dFpro + 1)/(d + 1).
  • A small coherent rotation error θ gives an average infidelity of about θ²/6 for one qubit; a conditional phase error δφ on a CZ gives about 0.15 δφ².
  • Binomial uncertainty of an estimated probability: σ = √(p(1 − p)/N).

Charge physics

  • Constant-interaction addition energy: Eadd = EC + ΔE, with EC = e²/CΣ. The plunger spacing of Coulomb peaks is ΔV = Eadd/(eα).
  • Lever arm from Coulomb diamonds: α = |mSmD/(mS − mD)| from the two edge slopes, or VSD at the diamond tip divided by its width in gate voltage.
  • Fermi function: f(E) = 1/(1 + eE/kBT). A thermally broadened Coulomb peak has FWHM = 3.53 kBTe/(eα) in gate voltage.
  • Two-level charge system with tunnel coupling tc: splitting Ω = √(ε² + 4tc²); polarization P = ½[1 + (ε/Ω) tanh(Ω/2kBT)].
  • Exchange in the Hubbard picture at the symmetric point: J ≈ 4tc²/U.
